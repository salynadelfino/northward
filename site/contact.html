What we can’t do

We can’t advise you on your own immigration position — not by email, not on WhatsApp, not informally. Giving immigration advice in the UK is regulated, and we are not registered to give it.

What that means in practice: ask us how the process generally works and we will tell you, and point you at the official source. Ask us what you should do about your situation and we will tell you honestly that we can’t answer it, and where to take it instead.

For advice on your own position, speak to an adviser regulated by the Immigration Advice Authority.

About Northward

What is Northward, exactly?

A career network. We publish practical guidance on building a healthcare career in the UK, and we keep a register of Indian healthcare professionals. When a UK employer we work with is recruiting, we contact the people on the Register whose profile fits.

We are not an immigration consultancy, a visa company or a job board, and we do not represent ourselves as any of those.

Do you guarantee a job or a visa?

No, and treat anyone who does with real suspicion. We can tell you how the process works and let you know when a relevant opportunity comes up. Whether you get the role is between you and the employer, and whether you get the visa is between them and the Home Office.

Do I have to be ready to move now?

No. That is rather the point of the Register. Plenty of people join while still studying, still preparing for an exam, or simply thinking about it for two or three years’ time. We would rather know you early.

Which professions do you cover?

Qualified healthcare professionals — nurses, doctors, and allied health professionals such as physiotherapists, radiographers, biomedical scientists, operating department practitioners and occupational therapists. Whether a specific route is open to you depends on your profession, your qualification and the UK rules at the time.

Cost

What does it cost to join the Register?

Nothing, at any point. It is also unlawful in the UK to charge a work-seeker a fee for finding them work, so if anyone in this sector asks you for one, that tells you something important about them.

What does the pathway itself cost?

Fees are payable to your regulator, your English test provider and UK Visas and Immigration, and they change. Those are paid by you, to them, not to us. The free guide itemises the main ones and flags the ones people discover late.

The pathway

How long does it take to move to the UK?

For most professions, realistically somewhere between a year and two years from a standing start — registration, exams, finding a role and the visa. It varies a lot by profession and by how much of the preparation you have already done. The pathway page sets out the stages.

What English level do I need?

There are usually two separate requirements: one set by your UK regulator for registration, and one set by the Home Office for the visa. They are not the same and they are assessed differently. Check both at source before booking anything — GOV.UK lists the approved tests.

Can you advise me on my visa situation?

No. Giving immigration advice in the UK is regulated, and we are not registered to give it. We explain how the process generally works and point you at the official source. For advice on your own position, speak to an adviser regulated by the Immigration Advice Authority.

Can I come to the UK as a care worker?

Not from overseas. Recruitment into care worker and senior care worker roles closed to overseas applicants in July 2025, and the skill threshold for sponsored work rose to degree level. Registered healthcare professions remain eligible, which is what Northward covers. As always, check the current position on GOV.UK rather than taking our word for it.

Is the UK definitely a good move?

Not necessarily, and we would rather say so. It can mean a significant change in pay and in the kind of work you do, but it also means cost, distance from family, a slow and expensive process, and starting again somewhere the systems are unfamiliar. It suits some people enormously and others not at all. Understand it properly, then decide.

The Register

What happens after I join?

We confirm we have you, review your profile, and keep you informed. When a relevant opportunity comes up we contact you directly. If nothing matches for a while, we will say so rather than go quiet — and we will not invent something to keep you engaged.

How often will you contact me?

Occasionally, and only with something worth your time: a new guide, a rule change that affects you, or a relevant opportunity. If that is still too much, tell us and we will only contact you about opportunities.

What is the difference between the guide and the Register?

The guide asks for three or four details and puts you on our mailing list. The Register asks about your qualification, experience and UK readiness, because we cannot match you to anything without knowing those. Downloading the guide does not put you on the Register.

What do you do with my details?

We hold them so we can tell you about relevant opportunities, and we share them with a UK employer only once you have said you want to be introduced to a specific role. We do not sell them and we do not pass them on to anyone else.

How do I come off the Register?

Reply to any message from us, or email hello@northward.co. We remove your details the same day and confirm that we have.

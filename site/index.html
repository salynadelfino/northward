What we do

Three things, done well

We teach the route

Every step from NMC application to your PIN, written plainly and kept current with the rules. Free to read, free to download, no account needed.

We connect nurses with employers

We work with UK healthcare employers recruiting internationally educated nurses. Nurses on our register are the first to hear when a role opens.

We stay with you

The route takes eighteen months to two years. We keep in touch across it and come to you when a role genuinely suits your stage and experience.

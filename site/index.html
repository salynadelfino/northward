Who this is for

Qualified nurses who want to work in the UK.

You might have a GNM or a BSc. You might be a year out of college, or eight years into a job you know inside out. You might have passed your CBT already — or you might still be working out whether any of this is realistic.

All of that is fine. Nurses come to the UK through hospitals, nursing homes, community teams and specialist services, and the setting that suits you depends as much on what you want next as on what you’ve done so far.

You won’t find vacancies listed here — we’re not a job board. What we do is explain how the route works, and get in touch when an employer we work with has something that fits. Joining the Register takes about ten minutes, and there’s no expectation that you move any time soon.

No rush

Not ready to move yet? That’s okay.

Maybe you’re still studying. Maybe you’re building experience, or saving, or waiting on an exam date. Maybe the UK is just something you’ve started thinking about.

Joining the Register keeps you connected until you’re ready. Plans change, and so does what employers are looking for. There’s no advantage in waiting until you think you qualify.

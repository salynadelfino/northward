What changed

Two rule changes that decide whether this route is open to you

Both happened recently. Neither was well explained.

8 January 2026

The English requirement went up, and almost nobody has told you

Skilled Worker applications now need CEFR B2 in all four components — reading, writing, speaking and listening. It was B1.

This is separate from the NMC’s own English requirement, and it has caught out a lot of nurses who planned around the old bar.

Read what B2 means per component →

22 July 2025

Care worker roles closed to overseas applicants. Nursing did not.

Overseas recruitment into care worker and senior care worker roles is closed, and the skill threshold rose to degree level.

Registered nursing remains fully eligible on the Health and Care Worker route. That is the whole reason this site is about nursing and nothing else.

What the route costs

£1,800–£3,500

All in, spread across roughly two years — NMC fees, English test, CBT, OSCE, visa and travel. The OSCE and its resit fee are the part people budget for last and regret most.

Questions

The ones people actually ask

Do you charge nurses anything?

No — not for guides, not for registration, not at any point. It is also unlawful in the UK to charge someone a fee for finding them work, so be careful of anyone who asks.

Do you guarantee a job or a visa?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. We work with UK employers who hire internationally educated nurses, and our registered members hear about roles first. That is access, not a promise.

How long does the whole route take?

Realistically eighteen months to two years — see the six steps above. Most of that waiting is time you can spend working and saving, which is why starting the English test now matters more than anything else you could do this month.

Can I come as a care worker instead?

Not from overseas. Recruitment into care worker and senior care worker roles closed to overseas applicants on 22 July 2025. Registered nursing is open, which is why this site is about nursing only.

Can I bring my family?

Dependants are permitted on the Health and Care Worker route. The rules on this change, so we link the authority rather than paraphrase it: GOV.UK — Health and Care Worker visa.

What do you do with my data?

We hold it so we can tell you about matching roles, share it with a UK employer only once you have said yes, and delete it the day you ask. The full detail is in the privacy notice.

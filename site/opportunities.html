Every listing states its registration and sponsorship position explicitly. Where an employer has not confirmed sponsorship for a role, we say that rather than imply it.

Two things we will not do

We will not imply every visitor can apply. Eligibility depends on your profession, your registration status and the visa rules at the time. A role being listed does not mean it is open to you.

We will not imply sponsorship is guaranteed. Sponsorship is the employer’s to offer, under Home Office rules, and not every role carries it.

Registering your interest in a listing is exactly that — an expression of interest. It is not an application, and we will tell you plainly where you stand.

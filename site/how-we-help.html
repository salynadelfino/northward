How we help

Teaching the route, and finding the role that fits.

The route

Six steps, eighteen months to two years

These are realistic durations, not best cases. Anyone telling you three months is selling you something.

  1. English test

    1–3 months

    IELTS or OET. The only step entirely in your hands, and the one that takes longest to redo — which is why it is first.

  2. NMC application and CBT

    2–4 months

    Your account, your evidence, and the computer-based test. The CBT can be booked quickly; revision is where the time goes.

  3. Test of Competence invitation

    1–3 months

    The NMC reviews your application and invites you to the practical part of the Test of Competence.

  4. Job offer and Certificate of Sponsorship

    1–4 months

    A UK employer offers you a role and issues your Certificate of Sponsorship. This is the stage the register exists for.

  5. Visa and travel

    3–8 weeks

    Your Health and Care Worker visa application, then arrival. Your employer handles the sponsorship side.

  6. OSCE, then your PIN

    6–12 weeks

    Sat in the UK once you are here. Pass it and the NMC issues your PIN — you are a registered nurse in the UK.

After you register

We are looking for the right role, not the first one

  1. You register, once

    Ten minutes. Your qualification, your English, where you are with the NMC, and when you could realistically move.

  2. We hold your details and keep them current

    There may be no matching role this month, or next. That is normal, and we will say so rather than go quiet. When something changes — on your side or ours — we update your record.

  3. We introduce you when the role fits

    A real vacancy with a real employer, matched to your experience and your stage. We would rather introduce you to one role that suits you than five that do not.

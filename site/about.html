About us

We kept hearing the same questions, and the same wrong answers.

Northward Care is a small team working between India and the UK, trying to make one difficult thing a bit less difficult.

Why we exist

If you’ve spent any time in a WhatsApp group about UK nursing, you already know the problem. One person says the CBT takes three months. Someone else says a year. A friend’s cousin paid an agent and never heard back. The rules move, the advice doesn’t keep up, and the people who need it most are the ones with the least time to sit and work it out.

There is a lot of noise around this route, and some of it is deliberate. We wanted to build something quieter: a place where the information is current, the source is named, and nobody is being sold anything.

The other half of it is connections. We work with UK employers who recruit internationally educated nurses, across hospitals, nursing homes and specialist services. So when a role opens that suits someone on our Register, we hear about it early and we can make the introduction. That is the part that is genuinely hard to do on your own from India, and it is the part we are useful for.

Who we are

A small team split between India and the UK. Between us we have worked on both sides of this — UK healthcare recruitment and sponsorship on one side, the Indian nursing route on the other — and we have seen enough of these applications to know where they usually come unstuck.

We would rather introduce ourselves properly here, with real names, than put up stock photographs and invented job titles. That page is coming.

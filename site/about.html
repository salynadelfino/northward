About

Going abroad isn’t just about getting on a plane.

It’s about knowing where you’re going — and why.

Why Northward exists

Most of what is written online about building a healthcare career in the UK is either trying to sell you something or is three rule changes out of date. Usually both. People make decisions worth years of their life and a great deal of money on the strength of a WhatsApp forward.

Northward exists to help Indian healthcare professionals make informed decisions about a UK career, understand what comes next, and connect with relevant opportunities when the time is right.

That is genuinely it. We are not an immigration consultancy, we are not a visa company, and we are not a job board. We are a career network: you tell us your professional profile once, we keep you informed, and we come to you when something fits.

What we believe

You should understand the process

Not just the next step someone wants you to take. The whole thing, including the parts that are slow and expensive.

The UK is not automatically the right move

For some people it is the wrong move at the wrong time. We would rather say so than sign someone up.

Nobody should pay to be found work

It is unlawful in the UK to charge a work-seeker a fee for finding them work. Treat any request for one as a warning.

Being early is not the same as being behind

Final-year students and people two years out belong on the Register as much as anyone with a visa appointment booked.

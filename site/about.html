About us

Helping you take your next step.

Northward Care connects talented healthcare professionals in India with opportunities in the UK — with the experience, connections and guidance to help make the journey simpler.

Who we are

A team on both sides of the journey

We’re a team based across India and the UK, and together we share experience in UK healthcare recruitment, sponsorship and international talent placement. We’ve spent years building relationships with UK healthcare employers and understanding what it takes to move and support nurses from India into the UK.

We know the people, the process and the journey — because we’re part of it.

Why we exist

There’s a lot of information out there. Not all of it is accurate.

Moving to the UK to build a career can be complicated. There’s a lot of information out there, but not all of it is accurate. Navigating registration, sponsorship, recruitment and relocation can feel overwhelming, and we’ve seen first-hand how easily good candidates get lost in the process. So we created Northward Care to make the journey clearer, more accessible and more connected.

We believe everyone deserves a fair opportunity to build a better future. We happen to have the connections to help make that next step possible.

How we work

No false promises. Just the right information at the right time.

Moving your career to another country is a big decision. You should understand the route, know what you’re working towards, and only hear from us when there’s something genuinely relevant to you.

Clear information

We explain the process in plain language, without the jargon or guesswork. When something is subject to change, we point you towards the official source.

Real opportunities

We work with UK employers who are actively hiring. We’ll contact you when there’s a genuine opportunity that matches your experience and profile, not just to keep you on a list.

No pressure

Joining the Northward Register doesn’t commit you to anything. You decide if and when moving to the UK is right for you.

Stay connected

Your plans, experience and availability can change. Keep your profile up to date, and we can make better matches when the right opportunity comes along.

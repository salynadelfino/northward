What happens next

Three things, in order

  1. We confirm we have you

    Straight away, by email. If something on your record needs clarifying, we will ask once.

  2. We review what you have

    Against what UK employers actually ask for. If there is a gap, we will tell you what it is rather than leave you guessing.

  3. We keep in touch

    Monthly, whether or not there is a role. When one fits, you hear from us directly.

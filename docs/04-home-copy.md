## 5 · What changed

> **The English requirement went up in January, and almost nobody has told you.**
>
> Since 8 January 2026, Skilled Worker applications need CEFR **B2** in all four
> components — reading, writing, speaking and listening. It was B1. This is
> separate from the NMC's own English requirement, and it has caught out a lot
> of nurses who planned around the old bar.
>
> *Read what B2 means per component →*

Second item, shorter, because it stops the wrong people registering:

> **Care worker roles closed to overseas applicants in July 2025.** Registered
> nursing did not. If you are a registered nurse, the route is open — that is
> the whole reason this site is about nursing and nothing else.

## 10 · FAQ

1. **Do you charge nurses anything?** No — not for guides, not for registration,
   not at any point. It is also unlawful in the UK to charge someone a fee for
   finding them work.
2. **Do you guarantee a job or a visa?** No, and be careful of anyone who does.
   We work with UK employers who hire internationally educated nurses, and our
   registered members hear about roles first. That is access, not a promise.
3. **How long does the whole route take?** Realistically eighteen months to two
   years. See the six steps above.
4. **Can I come as a care worker instead?** Not from overseas. That route closed
   on 22 July 2025. Registered nursing is open, which is why this site is about
   nursing only.
5. **Can I bring my family?** Dependants are permitted on the Health and Care
   Worker route. The rules on this change — GOV.UK is the authority and we link
   it rather than paraphrase it.
6. **What do you do with my data?** Hold it so we can tell you about matching
   roles, share it with a UK employer only once you have said yes, and delete it
   the day you ask. Full detail in the privacy notice.

Note question 4 and 5's construction: state the published position, link the
source, do not interpret it for the individual (guardrail §3).
